The challenge

Sales depended on seasonal demand and a few products. The brand wanted a dependable monthly baseline more than three times higher than where it started.

Takeaway

A phased keyword plan, from high intent to broad reach, turned a seasonal account into steady catalog-wide growth.

What we did

  • Prioritized products by real shopper demand
  • Grouped keywords by intent: product, benefit, generic and root
  • Started with tightly relevant product terms, then expanded to benefit and generic terms
  • Reduced reliance on one seasonal, higher-ticket product

The challenge

A good product with a main image that did not convert traffic efficiently. The goal was more revenue from the same traffic.

Takeaway

A clearer, more premium first impression lifted conversion without buying more traffic.

What we did

  • Ran a main image A/B test: control against an optimized version
  • Kept the jar as the hero and added premium packaging behind it
  • Called out the key benefits: airtight seal, premium aluminum, lightweight

The challenge

The main image showed a rope and handles, but not the accessories or the benefits, so it undersold the product.

Takeaway

Change one thing, measure it, and let the data pick the image.

What we did

  • A/B tested two main images with the same rope and handles
  • Version B added the accessory pouch and feature icons
  • Confirmed every claim and accessory with the brand first
