FAQ

Questions before you reach out.

What does EDAD Advertising do?

EDAD Advertising is an Amazon growth agency that specializes in listing optimization and PPC advertising (Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display) for brands selling on Amazon.

How much does EDAD Advertising cost?

Every project is quoted individually. Pricing depends on your catalog size, ad spend and goals, and you receive a written proposal after a free audit.

How soon will I see results?

Most brands see early listing and campaign improvements within the first weeks, while rank and efficiency build over time. EDAD Advertising sends weekly reports so you can track the trend.

Do I keep control of my Amazon account?

Yes. Your Amazon account always stays yours. EDAD Advertising works through access you grant, and you can remove it at any time.

Does EDAD Advertising work with new products?

Yes. The product launch service is built for new products with no rank or reviews yet, using full-funnel launch campaigns.

What does EDAD Advertising need to get started?

Read-only or advertising access to your Amazon account, your product catalog and your goals. The free audit shows exactly what is needed.

How often will I get reports?

Weekly. Each report uses plain numbers and ends with a clear next step, so you always know what changed and why.

How do I book a free audit?

Use any Book an Audit button or send the form on the contact page. EDAD Advertising replies within 24 hours.
