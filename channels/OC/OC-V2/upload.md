## Title

The Day a Wave of Molasses Hit Boston (1919)

## Description

On 15 January 1919, a 50-foot steel tank burst in Boston's North End and sent a wave of molasses through the streets. Twenty-one people died. What happened next changed how America builds. Sources below.
